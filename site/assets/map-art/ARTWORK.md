# Map artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. These atlases are project-owned assets; the application uses measured per-sprite crop bounds from map-sprite-bounds.js through map-sprites.js. Environmental and interior artwork is orthographic overhead. Location markers intentionally use perspective. Preserve transparent pixels and cell order when replacing an atlas, then measure the new bounds. Do not assume equal grid cells: the generated images have nonuniform gutters. Rendering, previews and heightmap sampling share the same centered crop.

Files:
- environment-atlas.png: 6 × 6, 36 environment parts.
- interiors-atlas.png: 6 × 6, 36 furniture and dungeon parts.
- locations-atlas.png: 4 × 4, 16 perspective landmark markers.
