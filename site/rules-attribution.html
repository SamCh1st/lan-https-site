The starter library adapts mechanical data into structured records and original, abbreviated summaries. It is a curated reference, not the complete SRD. Each card links to the relevant official rules. No endorsement by Wizards of the Coast is implied.

Revised fifth edition (2024) is the default. New 2014 or custom-rule campaigns do not receive the revised starter library. Existing 2024 campaigns receive the character-option reference sections once; existing class references are grouped under Classes. Campaign notes and custom cards are preserved.

Merchant defaults use listed equipment costs and SRD magic-item rarity values. Official listed prices are converted through gold: 1 official GP remains 1 site GP; 1 official SP becomes 10 site SP; 1 official CP becomes 1 site SP. The DM controls sale values and shop prices. Custom prices are preserved. This site uses the requested house currency conversion: 100 copper = 1 silver; 100 silver = 1 gold.
