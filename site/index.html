Dice & Table Rules

—

Local rolls, visible on this device only. Announce the total to the table. This tray does not apply damage or spend resources.

    At a glance · revised fifth edition

    On your turn: movement up to your Speed and one action; one bonus action if a feature permits it. A reaction needs a trigger and refreshes at the start of your next turn.

    Check or save: d20 + ability modifier + proficiency when applicable, against a DC. An attack targets AC. Ties meet the target. Advantage and disadvantage cancel; neither stacks.

    A critical hit doubles damage dice, not fixed bonuses. A natural 20 does not automatically succeed on every ability check.

    Spell save DC = 8 + proficiency + spellcasting modifier. Maintain only one concentration effect. On a turn, you can expend only one spell slot to cast a spell, unless a specific rule overrides this.

    Short rest: normally 1 hour; spend Hit Dice for healing. Long rest: normally 8 hours; recover HP, spent Hit Dice and eligible resources. Apply interruption rules and class exceptions.

    Official revised fifth-edition rules · Campaign house rules take precedence at your table.
