# Campaign files

## Download a campaign

On the main page, use **Download campaign** on a campaign you created. The site
prepares a ZIP containing its records, maps (including archived maps), connections,
scene settings, artwork, referenced local audio, inventory quantities, equipment
links, chat history, and your personal notes. Referenced catalog art is copied as
actual image files. Catalog installation markers prevent duplicate seeded cards.

Only the campaign creator can export the complete campaign, which includes DM
secrets. Other players' personal notes, account passwords, sessions, and the
site-wide audio library are not exported. Audio files referenced by campaign data
are included. External website links remain links. The receiving site needs a
compatible version of the application; AI model installations are separate.

## Import a campaign

1. Sign in and click **Import campaign** beside **New campaign** on the main page.
2. Choose the downloaded ZIP and confirm.
3. Wait for **Campaign imported**. The copy appears in the campaign list.

Import always creates a separate campaign owned by your signed-in account. Record,
map, and upload IDs are rebuilt so links stay inside the copy. The original is
unchanged. Importing the same file twice creates two separate copies. The maximum
ZIP size is 512 MB; invalid or damaged files are rejected before creating records.

## Reconnect players

Accounts are not transferred or automatically enrolled. Invite players through the
campaign's normal invitation controls. Once they accept, return to the main page
and choose **Assign players** on the imported campaign. Match the old player names
to accepted accounts, then save. This restores character ownership, saved map
positions, visited maps, and revealed character markers for those players.

Your copy initially keeps private imported chat history visible to you. Review
private-chat audiences before sharing old conversations with returning players.
Historical AI rewards are not replayed. Opening checks tied to old accounts may
need to be rolled again after reconnecting players.
